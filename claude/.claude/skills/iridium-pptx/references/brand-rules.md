## Naming rules

- **Iridium Certus** must never appear as just "Certus".
- Iridium Certus is a service platform: midband = Iridium Certus 20, 100; broadband = Iridium Certus 200, 350, 700.
- Iridium Certus 1400 — approved language only: "The Iridium network is capable of supporting speeds up to 1408 Kbps. Future development of Iridium Certus 1400 service is dependent on market demand."
- **BCX / SFX are internal terms.** Externally: BCX = Iridium Certus™ 9810, SFX = Iridium Certus™ 9770.
- **Iridium NEXT** is retired except when referring to the historic launch campaign. The constellation is "the Iridium constellation" or "Iridium network" (optionally "enhanced"/"upgraded"). The satellites are just "Iridium satellites". Avoid "Block 1" in public communications.

## Logos

- Corporate logo: corporate communications, presentations, graphics.
- Iridium Connected logo: only for partner products enabled by the Iridium network.
- Retired, never use: Iridium Everywhere logo; Iridium Certus identifier/mobius strip/connected logo; Iridium NEXT identifier (historic references only).
- High-res logo and satellite assets are on guideline slide 3 of the original template and on IFP.

## Third parties

Don't use another company's product or trademark in a way that could be construed as endorsement of (or by) Iridium — this is a legal liability. Avoid identifiable third-party products and named individuals in marketing material.
